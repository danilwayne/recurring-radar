# RecurringRadar — Product Vision

## What we are building

A financial decision assistant for founders and operators that tells you exactly which software to cancel, when, and how much you will recover — and then proves it happened.

## Who it is for

Founders and operators of small-to-medium companies (5–200 employees) who:
- Pay for more software than their teams actually use
- Lack the time or process to audit subscriptions regularly
- Want proof that a tool paid for itself

## What makes it different

Most subscription trackers show you what you pay. RecurringRadar tells you what to do.

The distinction is architectural: the Recommendation Engine runs deterministic rules against your subscription data and surfaces the highest-value action available today. The user does not browse charts to find waste — the product delivers the decision.

## The economy loop

```
Cadastro → Engine → Recomendação → Ação → Registro → ROI
```

1. User adds subscriptions (Assinaturas)
2. Engine analyzes usage, cost, duplication, renewal risk (background)
3. Radar surfaces the #1 decision today
4. User acts (confirm/snooze/dismiss)
5. Economy is recorded with subscription name, amount, and date
6. Impacto proves the accumulated value delivered

## The three pillars

| Pillar | Screen | Question answered |
|---|---|---|
| Find | Radar | What should I do right now? |
| Feed | Assinaturas | What am I paying for? |
| Prove | Impacto | What has RecurringRadar done for me? |

## The competitive positioning

**Not:** Rocket Money (consumer), Blissfully/Torii (enterprise IT)
**Yes:** The founder-facing SaaS spend optimizer — simpler than enterprise SMPs, more intelligent than spreadsheets.
